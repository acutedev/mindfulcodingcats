WHAT HAPPENS IN CLASS?

From “I have an idea!” to “I made this!” ✨

Each class is built around creating. Students learn a concept, experiment with it, and turn their own ideas into something that works.

💡Imagine

Start with an idea, question, interest or problem worth exploring.

🧙‍♀️Learn & experiment

Explore coding, AI and creative technology with guidance from Teacher Lana.

🚀Create & grow

Build a working project, solve problems and keep improving it.

The goal isn’t just to finish an exercise. It’s to experience the joy of turning imagination into something real.
