😻 Why Mindful Coding Cats?

We're not like any other coding school out there! We're all about making coding fun and engaging for your child, while teaching them ✨mindfulness✨ and the skills they need to succeed in the future. 🧘🏻‍♀️💻🚀

Our online AI and coding classes are specifically designed for children ages 8-14 (grades 2-8) who love their digital devices, computer games and want to learn to code. In our fun and engaging classes, students will not only master the fundamentals of computer science but also will be developing and practicing mindfulness skills that will help them focus, learn, and be creative. 💡🎨🧙‍♀️

Your child will develop problem-solving, critical thinking, and creativity skills as they work on fun projects that challenge them and help them grow as learners. 🤖🧮🧬

Plus, your child will complete each class with a working creative project or game, as well as suggested challenges to work on between classes. 🎮🧩

Even more, we believe that learning doesn't just happen in the classroom - that's why we provide your child with all the resources and support they need to continue coding at home 🏠! With our help, your child can become a coding wizard 🧙‍♂️ in no time!
