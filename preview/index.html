and I'm so excited to teach your child the wonders of AI and coding! 💻

😻 Why Mindful Coding Cats?

We're not like any other coding school out there! We're all about making coding fun and engaging for your child, while teaching them ✨mindfulness✨ and the skills they need to succeed in the future. 🧘🏻‍♀️💻🚀

Our online AI and coding classes are specifically designed for children ages 8-14 (grades 2-8) who love their digital devices, computer games and want to learn to code. Students master computer science fundamentals while developing mindfulness skills that help them focus, learn, and be creative. 💡🎨🧙‍♀️

Your child develops problem-solving, critical thinking, and creativity while completing working creative projects and games. 🎮🧩

We believe learning doesn't just happen in the classroom, so we provide resources and support to continue coding at home. 🏠
